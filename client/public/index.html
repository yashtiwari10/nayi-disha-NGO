Girl Child Education Fund

From Fields to Classrooms

Priya, 12, from a small village in Bihar, had stopped attending school to help her family. Through the Girl Child Education Fund, she received a bicycle, school kit, and her fees were covered. Today, she ranks in the top 5 of her class and dreams of becoming a doctor.

"Because of Nayi Disha, my daughter can now go to school. We never thought this was possible for us." — Meena Devi, Patna
